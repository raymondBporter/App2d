Downloaded September 20, 2026; original glTF files preserved. The shared color
atlas is not required by this skeleton-only experiment and was not downloaded.
Source SHA-256 hashes are included in generated `motion.json`.

The `faces/happy.png` and `faces/grumpy.png` images are project-authored
placeholders copied from the quadruped study, not part of the Quaternius pack.
Their editable recipe is `assets/quadruped/faces.json` and the existing renderer
is `scripts/build_doodle_faces.py`.
