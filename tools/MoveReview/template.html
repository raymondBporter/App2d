Player move review

Every player move rebuilt on the authored Person rig in the walk and run style, rendered by the game's own drawing code. Each card loops the move; use ½× and ¼× to see the key poses, Game size to judge it at the player's in-game height, and the reference strip where a move borrows from the RGS stick-figure set. Mark each move Keep, Tweak or Redo and leave a note. Notes save as you go and Claude reads them back.

Decisions I made for you to check

  1. Climb is a back view, reached by turning. With no perspective the turn just slides the shoulders and hips across as their axis spins, swapping near and far on the way; the face gives way to the back of the head past profile and the sheath swaps to across-the-back. The turn off the ladder is the same keys reversed.
  2. Wall grip faces away from the wall. Gameplay already turns the player away from the wall, as in the RGS wall slide: back and one foot on the wall, near hand braced behind, weapon hand free.
  3. Jump has no crouch. Gameplay leaves the ground on the button press, so the clip opens on the push instead of an anticipation squat.
  4. Sword draw. The hand finds the hilt over the shoulder, and pulling the blade out becomes the overhead wind-up. The downward stab damages from frame one, so its draw is a two-frame whip.
  5. Gun shot is upper body only. It keys only chest, head and arms, so it layers on aim, walk or run legs. The three Layered cards prove it with no new animation.
  6. Sheath hidden behind the back. The hilt now sits below the shoulder line behind the torso, since a guard at neck height read as part of the neck. In profile only the scabbard tip shows below the back, and the draw reaches behind the shoulder for it. The sword is 0.82 units.
