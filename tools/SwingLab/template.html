Swing Lab

The player's sword slash, rebuilt swoosh first and compared one change at a time against today's clip. Each card is rendered at 60 fps by the game's own drawing code, and the swoosh is the live C# trail, fed one blade per frame the way the game would feed it. Try ½× and ¼×, scrub through the frames, turn the swoosh off to see the bare body, and use On twos to see the swing stepped at 30 fps. Mark each card and leave a note; notes save as you go and Claude reads them back.

Round 8: the side cut and combo are in the game

  • New this round: "In the game". The first card plays the game's own clips back to back, the way a mashed button chains them: side cut, backhand, forehand, backhand, put away. They were cut out of the combo you picked, so it should play exactly like the combo card, apart from the idle breathing. Frame for frame it renders identically.
  • How it chains. Each press during a swing queues the next. Pressed again while the put-away still holds the sword, you get the next swing. After the sword is back, it draws again with the side cut. Mashed, contacts are 0.25 s apart.
  • The hit. Each swing's hit is now a rectangle fixed to the player, worked out from the game sword's sweep and saved in the hero's entity file, where the editor can change it. It no longer follows the blade.
  • The swoosh. The live trail now draws in the game, behind the player, with the follow-up mark on backhands.
  • Still open. The cartoon sword (the game still uses today's sword, so the game's hit rectangles are a little shorter than the ones shown here), the grip at the far-side wind-up, and the final hit rectangle size.
