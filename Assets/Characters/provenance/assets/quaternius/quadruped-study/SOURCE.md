The integrated preview uses evaluated bone positions from all 12 wolf actions,
listed in `config/quadruped-library.json`. The original study used four of them.
The ink hound is a new primitive drawing over those positions. The original
wolf mesh is not included in the browser preview. `StickHound.blend` retains
the hidden donor mesh and rig plus a separate collection of weighted primitives.
