Swing Lab

The player's sword slash, rebuilt swoosh first and compared one change at a time against today's clip. Each card is rendered at 60 fps by the game's own drawing code, and the swoosh is the live C# trail, fed one blade per frame the way the game would feed it. Try ½× and ¼×, scrub through the frames, turn the swoosh off to see the bare body, and use On twos to see the swing stepped at 30 fps. Mark each card and leave a note; notes save as you go and Claude reads them back.

What changed from today's slash

  • The arc comes first. Each key places the hand on a circle round the shoulder and says how the wrist holds the blade. The arm IK and the body follow from that, and the body's lean carries the arc forward.
  • The blade sits across the fist. It's held at 45–80° to the forearm in every frame (except the whip's one deliberate broken frame), never laid along it. Today's clip spends 10 of its 22 frames under 40°.
  • Timing. One frame to coil, two across (three in one variant), contact on frame 3 (50 ms). The tip is fastest on the step into contact and loses 90% or more of its speed within two frames. Today's is fastest 5 frames before its strike and loses 7%.
  • Combo art. The rising follow-up uses the same swoosh construction with a different split, so it reads as the same family.
  • Not decided here: the damage window (gameplay still uses 0.10–0.27 s), the first swing from the sheath, and hit feedback.
